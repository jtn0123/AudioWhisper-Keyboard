The library opens at nine on weekdays.
Most visitors come in the late morning, after the first trains have arrived and the coffee shops on the corner have emptied out.
Upstairs, the reading room is quiet.
Long tables run the length of the hall, each with a green lamp and a row of power sockets that were added during the renovation two years ago.
Please return books to the desk.
On Saturdays there is a short talk for children, usually about animals, the weather, or how bridges are built.
The building closes at six.
If you need a card, bring something with your address on it, and the staff will set one up in a few minutes.
Thank you for visiting.
